## Video direction

- Palette (from frame.md, brand-remixed broadside): canvas deep olive `#0F380F`, ink lime `#9BBC0F`, accent gold for earn moments, dark panels `#051208`. Pixel type by role: display Jacquard12 for headlines, body Silkscreen/PixelifySans for labels.
- Motion grammar: smooth long-tail eases, reveal each card line on its text cue (never front-load), slow push-ins over gameplay screenshots, held reads on earn stats and end card. Holds stay alive with subtle jitter at most — no lazy breathing, no drift.
- Rhythm: frames 1–4 reveal across the back half; frame 5 end card is a deliberate held read. No screensaver motion, no slideshow dumps.
- Negative list: no purple-blue AI gradients, no floating bokeh, no browser chrome/cursors, no generic stock shapes standing in for game art. Every visual is a real gameplay capture.
- Caption band: content lives in top ~83%; bottom ~17% kept clear.
