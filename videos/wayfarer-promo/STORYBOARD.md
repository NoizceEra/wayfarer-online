## Video direction

- **Palette system** (from `frame.md`): dark register throughout — `ink-black` #0A0E1A ground, `cream` #E1E8F0 text, single accent `fire-orange`→**#14F195 Solana green**. Frames 2 and 4 additionally use the three secondary Solana tokens (`solana-cyan` #03E1FF, `solana-purple` #9945FF, `solana-magenta` #DC1FFF) — but ONLY inside the faithfully-recreated real Agent/Arena panel chrome itself (that's the actual in-game palette), never as a second general-purpose accent on any other frame. Type by role only: Jacquard12 for every display/headline, PixelifySans for body/lead, Silkscreen for mono labels/stats — never raw family names.
- **Motion grammar + reveal model**: `power3` long-tail settle everywhere; the two blueprint-sanctioned overshoots (the comparison-split badge pop in Frame 5, the wordmark badge pop in Frame 6) are the ONLY bounce in the video — both "earn the punctuation" per their blueprint. Every frame reveals content on its spoken cue, never before; nothing dumps in the first 25% of any frame.
- **Rhythm / held-frame allocation**: Frames 1 and 6 are the bookend breathers — short, resolve early, then hold dead-still (real footage motion in Frame 1 is the only "aliveness," no manufactured jitter needed there). Frame 2 and Frame 4 are the two reveal climaxes (token→agent, then agent→arena) and carry the most motion. Frame 3 is a measured mid-tempo demo. Frame 5 is the symmetry showpiece — camera-static by its blueprint's own rule, letting the mirrored cards carry the energy.
- **Negative list**: no slideshow front-loading; no lazy breathing or back-half camera pan/push (real video footage's native motion is not "breathing" and is exempt); no second general accent color; no fabricated numerals or dollar figures anywhere — every stat shown is either real captured UI text (RATING 1000, W0·L0, the Agent panel's actual field labels) or an explicit placeholder, never an invented SOL amount or guaranteed-earnings figure; no generic stock "AI gradient" bokeh; no browser chrome / cursor except where a frame explicitly recreates real in-game UI.
