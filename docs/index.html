About

Wayfarer Online is a pixel-art action RPG inspired by the Game Boy aesthetic. Explore Thistle Town and the surrounding wilds, slay monsters, collect loot, craft gear, and team up with friends in real-time co-op.

The game runs entirely in your browser - no downloads, no installs. Works on desktop and mobile.

[+] Open World

Explore meadows, woods, ruins, and towns. Seamless overworld with no loading screens.

[+] Action Combat

[+] Deep Gear

Loot, equip, dye, and upgrade helmets, blades, charms, and more.

Controls

  • WASD / Arrow keys - Move
  • J / Space - Attack
  • E - Interact / Talk
  • 1-4 - Abilities
  • Q - Potion
  • B - Inventory / Equip
  • L - Quest Log
  • U - Crafting
  • Enter - Chat
  • Esc - Pause / Close panels
  • M - Toggle minimap size
  • V - Mailbox

On mobile: virtual joystick (bottom-left), action buttons (bottom-right), tap the hotbar for skills.

Co-op

From the title screen, choose Play Online to join the public persistent world, or Host Co-op to create a private room with a shareable code.

Party members share enemy aggro, loot drops are instanced per player, and experience is pooled. Up to 8 players per private room.

Economy

Wayfarer has a full player-driven economy:

  • Direct Trade - Right-click a player and request a trade. Both sides lock their offer, then confirm.
  • Market Board - List items for sale at notice boards in towns. Buy from other players 24/7.
  • Mail - Send items and gold to offline players.
  • Guild Bank - Deposit and withdraw gold for your guild.

  • Server: Colyseus (Node.js) - real-time game relay
  • Store: SQLite (WAL mode) - character persistence
  • Deploy: Vercel (client) + Railway (server)
